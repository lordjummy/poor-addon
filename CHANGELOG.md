# Changelog

## 0.16.0

- A song plays when you level up, and a chat line with a short ding tells you when a guildmate does (silent in dungeons and raids). Both can be turned off in the options. /poor ding plays the song.

## 0.15.0

- Shared quests, on the Groups tab: your quests that a guildmate has too, group quests first, with one click to ask them along.
- The dungeon list is WoW Forever's own: 33 dungeons with their level ranges, including the new ones and the split wings.

## 0.14.3

- The update notice now says to /reload after updating; a restart is not needed.

## 0.14.2

- The Roster shows which version of the addon each member runs.
- `/poor testnotice` shows what an update notice looks like, on your own screen only.

## 0.14.1

- No changes to how the addon works. A release to check that members on an older version are told about a newer one.

## 0.14.0

- New Home tab: one line per area showing what is going on right now, each opening its tab.
- Fewer tabs. Funds and the Board are together under Gold; Poorest, Race and Shame are together under Leaderboards. The alert log opens with a right-click on the PvP alert button.

## 0.13.0

Changes from an outside review of the addon.

- Recruiting is the officers' job unless they open it to members. A guild invite needs a whisper first, and a player only counts as approached once the whisper is sent.
- Loans: the borrower can no longer delete a debt. They can say it is repaid, and the lender confirms. Officers can clear old debts.
- Lotteries: the organiser cannot hold tickets, the winner confirms the payout, and a fund holding gold can only be deleted by its keeper.
- Deaths and repair bills are private unless you opt in, and the setup now says what is shared.
- The Roster shows who has the addon, and the Groups tab says how many guildmates will see a listing and can say it in guild chat.
- Names on the Groups tab and items on the Bank tab can be clicked to ask.
- Setup is four steps, opens the window when done, and asks a new alt only about that character.
- Tabs with nothing to show stay out of the sidebar. Guild bank characters no longer count on the Poorest board.

## 0.12.1

- Officers can keep recruiting to themselves: an "Only officers recruit" switch on the Recruit tab.

## 0.12.0

- Recruit tab: write your own recruiting message, with the player's name, class and level filled in. Officers can set the guild's default.
- Players approached by anyone in the guild are now marked for everyone, with who did it and when.

## 0.11.1

- Recruit tab lists all nine classes for both factions.

## 0.11.0

- New Recruit tab: the guild by class with the thin ones first, a wanted list set by officers, and a search for unguilded players of a class to whisper or invite.

## 0.10.0

- New Groups tab: post what you want to run and which roles you need, see who joins, invite them with one button. It also shows which online guildmates are in a dungeon's level range.

## 0.9.10

- PvP alerts: the sound, on-screen warning and map pin now fire only if you are in the same zone. Alerts from elsewhere are a quiet chat line. An option turns the full warning back on for every alert.

## 0.9.9

- Crafters tab: clicking a recipe opens a whisper to the crafter with the request already typed.

## 0.9.8

- The setup has a step for your professions: it shows which are shared and opens the ones not read yet with one button.

## 0.9.7

- Recipes are shared automatically. Once anyone in the guild has opened a profession's window, every member with that profession has their recipes read without opening anything, and newly learned recipes are added as you learn them.

## 0.9.6

- Tells you when a newer version is out: a chat line at login and a note in the window's title bar. The news is passed along between members, so it reaches you even if nobody on the new version is online.

## 0.9.5

- Crafters tab: professions are listed on the left; pick one to see its crafters, best skill first.

## 0.9.4

**Everyone needs this version: it cannot sync with older ones.**

- Fixes data not matching between members. Recipes, bank contents and alt links from other members were being thrown away, and funds could show the wrong keeper.
- Profession windows are recognised more reliably, so more recipes are picked up.

## 0.9.3

- A short setup appears the first time the addon runs: sharing your gold, your main character, PvP alerts. `/poor setup` shows it again.

## 0.9.2

- Sharing your gold is now off by default. The Poorest tab asks once whether you want to join the leaderboard.

## 0.9.1

- Adds the CurseForge project ID so apps recognise an installed copy.

## 0.9.0

First public beta.

- Roster, Poorest leaderboard, Events, Funds and lotteries, Race to 60, Board, Shame, Bank, Crafters and PvP alerts.
- Pledges: say what you are sending and it is recorded when it arrives.
